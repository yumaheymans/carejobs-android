# CareJobs.Now for Android

The [CareJobs.Now](https://carejobs.now) job board as an Android app: search open US healthcare jobs (nurses, CNAs, medical assistants, therapists, techs, pharmacy, physicians and caregivers) read every day from hospitals' and care employers' own career sites, with the full posting and the pay each employer states. Free, no ads, no account needed.

- Job search by kind of job, state, city and keywords, with posted pay
- Free weekly job alerts for your kind of job and state
- Pay by job and state, employer pages, new grad, travel, PRN and night shift jobs
- Apply on the employer's own site

## Download

Get `carejobs-now-1.0.0.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/yumaheymans/carejobs-android/releases/latest) and open it on your phone (Android 5.0 or later; allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks).

## Details

- Package: `now.carejobs.app`, version 1.0.0 (version code 1), minimum Android 5.0 (API 21)
- Built as a Trusted Web Activity of https://carejobs.now with [PWABuilder](https://www.pwabuilder.com); the site's [Digital Asset Links](https://carejobs.now/.well-known/assetlinks.json) file verifies the app
- Permissions: none. It asks for no internet, location, contacts or notification permission: the pages open in your installed Chrome (or another browser that supports Trusted Web Activities).
- Privacy policy: https://carejobs.now/legal/privacy
- Support: hello@carejobs.now

Other ways to use CareJobs.Now: https://carejobs.now/apps
